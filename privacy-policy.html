Overview

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect any personal data.

The Application "Shed It!" is a standalone utility designed to help musicians practice. It does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information.

All data and settings generated by the application (like tempo, selected chords, etc.) are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to any external servers.

The app does not use any third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or AdMob.
